Optimized 8-week plan
You're already ahead on AI and DSA, so I'd shift time to speed, fundamentals and communication.
Weeks 1-2 (speed and gaps):
Do 3 timed HackerRank sessions a week (2 problems in 55 minutes), and focus on hashing, greedy, DP, and strings.
Spend 1 hour a day on DBMS/SQL, OS, OOP, and networking.
Practise English for 20 minutes daily.
Weeks 3-4 (AI breadth):
Cover classical ML: regression, trees, feature engineering, and evaluation metrics.
Learn production topics: RAG evaluation, LLM deployment, and responsible/trustworthy AI, which IBM emphasises.
Skim watsonx and Granite.
Write and rehearse a 2-minute story for each of your 4 main projects.
Weeks 5-6 (communication):
Do 2 mock GDs a week.
Practise a spoken self-introduction and HR answers out loud, ideally recorded.
Give a peer a spoken explanation of MoE routing, GQA and KL-distillation in plain language.
Week 7 (mocks): Do 3 full coding tests plus English, and 2 mock interviews with a senior or friend.
Week 8 (polish): Do light daily practice, finish the resume placeholders, and rest before the drive.
